# Hero scroll-film layers

`bg.webp`, `clouds.webp`, `portal.webp` and `card1–3.webp` are the layers of the hero's scroll
film (src/sections/home/CinematicHero). They were taken from the hero template's own image set,
downloaded once and served from this project (not linked), and re-encoded from PNG to WebP
(about 0.6 MB in all, down from 14 MB). `portal` and `clouds` keep their transparency.

They are the template's artwork, not Visakram photography; replace them with approved imagery
if the hero ever moves away from this look.
