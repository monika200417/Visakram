# Eagle hero media

Used by the homepage hero (src/sections/home/EagleHero).

- `eagle-flight.webm` / `eagle-flight.mp4` — a 6 s clip of an eagle sweeping in and ending in a perched pose.
- `eagle-ground.webp` — the mist ground the clip plays over.
- `hero-city.webp` — the finished frame: a dark gray tower carrying the Visakram Builders sign above a coastal city at sunrise (1678 x 937). It has no eagle in it: the flight clip dissolves into it.

They are illustrative, AI-generated artwork supplied with the eagle landing demo — not photography of a
Visakram project and not a named building. Replace them with approved imagery when it exists; if the
still or the clip change, re-measure the eye positions in `src/sections/home/EagleHero/heroConfig.ts`.

`hero-city.webp` was replaced by the client-supplied picture (1678x937, same size as before, so the camera / dive numbers in heroConfig.ts still apply): the big tower on the right is dark gray, with a gold sign.
