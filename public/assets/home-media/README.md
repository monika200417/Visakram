# Homepage media — stock previews

`villas.jpg`, `residential-construction.jpg` and `featured-project.jpg` are stock photographs
from Unsplash (free to use under the Unsplash License), used so the "What We Build" and
"Featured Project" chapters can be reviewed with real imagery. They are **not photographs of
Visakram Builders projects**. Replace them with approved project photography (keep the names
or update src/data/homeMedia.ts and src/data/featuredProjectPreview.ts).

`proof-completed.png` and `proof-ongoing.webp` were supplied by the client for the "Completed
projects" and "Ongoing projects" cards of the statement chapter. Their provenance and licence
are not documented here: `proof-completed.png` carries a stock-agency watermark (a preview
image), so confirm it is licensed — or replace it with an approved photograph — before launch.

`residential-card.png` was supplied by the client as the picture for the Residential Construction card (src/data/homeMedia.ts). Its source and licence are not documented here; confirm them before launch. `residential-construction.jpg` (the earlier stock photograph of a site crew) is no longer used.
